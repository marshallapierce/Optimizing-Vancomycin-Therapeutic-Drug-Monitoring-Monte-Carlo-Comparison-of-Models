Key Notes:

  • Word counts are for original research articles; reviews may have higher limits
  • Abstracts typically require structured format (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion) unless noted
  • Figures/tables limits are combined totals; color figures often incur additional fees
  • Reference styles: AMA (American Medical Association) or Vancouver (numbered) are most common
  • Open Access fees (APC) are approximate and may vary by country/institution
  • Peer review is typically double-blind for anonymity
  • Decision times are initial editorial decisions; full review may take longer
  • Acceptance rates are approximate and can vary by article type

General Requirements Across Journals:

  • Original research with clear methodology
  • Ethical approval statements for human/animal studies
  • Conflict of interest disclosures
  • Patient consent for identifiable data
  • Statistical analysis section
  • Funding acknowledgments
